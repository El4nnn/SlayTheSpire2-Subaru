Subaru Mod · Full Change Log (0.1.0 → 0.2.35)

New Content
· Character: Subaru Natsuki
· 5 new cards: Incomplete E.M.T (1 cost · Skill · 8 Block, 12 upgraded), Puck (1 cost · Skill · 6 Block now and 6 next turn, 8/8 upgraded), Melakuera, the Arbitrator (1 cost · Attack · 6 damage + 6 Block, 8/8 upgraded), Muspel, the Stone Block (2 cost · Skill · 15 Block, 20 upgraded; costs 0 if Spirit Eater was played this turn), Celestia, the Passing Demon (2 cost · Attack · Exhaust · 1×N damage, N = times you dealt damage this combat, 3×N upgraded)
· Return by Death scene: the witch scene draws at random from 4 assets (one with its own voice); a Death that brings on Madness has a 50% chance to play a short video
