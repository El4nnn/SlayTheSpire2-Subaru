# 菜月昴 mod · 总更新内容（0.1.0 → 0.2.35）

> 📌 **active** ｜ 最后核对：2026-11-23 ｜ 累计更新（分类合并，无逐版对应）

## 一、新增内容

- **角色「菜月昴」**上线（0.1.0 首次发布）
- **新增 5 张卡**
  - 不完全E.M.T — 普通 · 1 费 · 技能 · 获得 8 格挡（升级 12）
  - 帕克 — 普通 · 1 费 · 技能 · 本回合 6 格挡、下回合再 6 格挡（升级 8/8）
  - 调停者·梅拉奎拉 — 普通 · 1 费 · 攻击 · 6 伤 + 6 格挡（升级 8/8）
  - 石块·姆斯佩尔 — 罕见 · 2 费 · 技能 · 15 格挡（升级 20）；本回合打出过[食精灵者]则费用为 0
  - 过路魔·塞蕾丝缇雅 — 罕见 · 2 费 · 攻击 · [消耗] · 1×N 伤害，N 为本场战斗造成过伤害的次数（升级 3×N）
- **死归演出素材池**：魔女登场画面从 4 张素材里随机抽（其中一张带独立语音，停留时长按各自语音长度）；会触发癫狂的那一次死归有 50% 概率改成播放一段视频
- **专属战斗内能量球底图**：手绘油画六芒星
- **悬停状态说明**：幼女使者、怠惰因子、傲慢、强欲的契约、贝蒂、癫狂、魔女遗香、浮空、魔女瘴气，部分带关联卡面预览
- **全文本英文化**：卡牌、遗物、能力、事件、时间线，按游戏语言自动切换
- **UI 主题色改为紫色**：名字、对话、地图绘制、瞄准线、卡背、卡组小卡、能量描边、遗物 / 药水池描边

## 三、修复

- 攻击牌打出后卡在屏幕上、伤害不结算
- 腐蚀牌在反复死归时被重复复制，牌组里越攒越多
- 腐蚀牌打出后死归，不再回到手牌，也不会多复制一张
- 进入休息处导致游戏崩溃
- 死归后手牌点不动，只能结束回合
- 魔女瘴气在回合结束被丢弃；现在留在手牌并按张数扣血
- 用掉药水后再死归会报错
- 被击杀瞬间血条显示 1 血；现在整个死亡动画期间显示 0
- 死归演出结束后画面残留紫色遮罩
- 魔女瘴气「在手牌中回合结束失去 1 点生命」不生效
- 用过的腐蚀牌仍能被自动打出、或从消耗堆取回
- 只能由其他牌生成的牌出现在卡牌奖励和商店中
- 「食精灵者」显示多余的「力量削减」图标

## 四、其他改动

- 死归演出改为受到致命伤害时立刻播放，不再等到下一回合开始
- 七件遗物的风味文字替换（傲慢因子 / 龙剑 / 阳剑 / 村雨 / 正梦 / 对话镜）
- 魔女契约事件的「拒绝」「签约」两页文案调整
- 模组描述补充多人模式警告与联系方式

# Subaru Mod · Full Change Log (0.1.0 → 0.2.35)

> Compiled from all 22 Workshop change notes.
> Where a card or mechanic was changed more than once, **only the final value is listed**.

## 1. New Content

- **New character: Subaru Natsuki** (first release 0.1.0)
- **5 new cards**
  - Incomplete E.M.T — Common · 1 cost · Skill · gain 8 Block (12 upgraded)
  - Puck — Common · 1 cost · Skill · 6 Block now and 6 Block next turn (8/8 upgraded)
  - Melakuera, the Arbitrator — Common · 1 cost · Attack · 6 damage + 6 Block (8/8 upgraded)
  - Muspel, the Stone Block — Uncommon · 2 cost · Skill · 15 Block (20 upgraded); costs 0 if Spirit Eater was played this turn
  - Celestia, the Passing Demon — Uncommon · 2 cost · Attack · Exhaust · 1×N damage, where N is the number of times you dealt damage this combat (3×N upgraded)
- **Return by Death scene pool**: the witch scene now draws from 4 assets (one with its own voice; screen time follows each clip); a Death that brings on Madness has a 50% chance to play a short video instead
- **Dedicated in-combat energy counter artwork**: hand-painted hexagram
- **Hover status tips**: Lolimancer, Sloth Factor, Pride, Greed Contract, Beatrice, Madness, Witch's Scent, Float, Witch Miasma — some with linked card previews
- **Full English localization**: cards, relics, powers, events and timeline nodes follow the game language
- **Purple UI theme**: name, dialogue, map drawing, targeting line, card back, deck entries, energy outline, relic and potion pool outlines

## 3. Fixes

- Attack cards got stuck on screen and their damage never resolved
- Corroded cards were duplicated on repeated Return by Death, piling up in the deck
- A played Corroded card no longer returns to hand or gets duplicated after a Return by Death
- Entering a rest site crashed the game
- Cards could not be played after Return by Death (only End Turn worked)
- Witch Miasma was discarded at end of turn; it now stays in hand and drains HP per card
- Rewinding after using a potion threw an error
- The health bar showed 1 HP on the killing blow; it now shows 0 for the whole death animation
- Purple overlay left on screen after the Return by Death scene
- Witch Miasma's "lose 1 HP at end of turn while in hand" did not work
- Used Corroded cards could still be auto-played or retrieved from the exhaust pile
- Cards that can only be generated by other cards showed up in card rewards and shops
- Spirit Eater displayed a redundant Strength-down icon

## 4. Other Changes

- The Return by Death scene now plays the moment lethal damage is taken instead of waiting for the next turn
- Flavor text replaced on seven relics (Pride Factor / Dragon Sword / Sun Sword / Murasame / True Dream / Dialogue Mirror)
- Text revised on the "Refuse" and "Sign" pages of the Witch Contract event
- Multiplayer warning and contact info added to the mod description
